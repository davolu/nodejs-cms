The connector platform

Connect an app. Get widgets.

Connectors are not just backend links — they power what you drop on the page. Link Google and you get a Sign-in button, a Sheets table, Drive files, a calendar. Link Mailchimp and you get a signup form. Credentials live in your own environment; you are prompted only when you connect something.

Connect Google→ Sign in with GoogleSheets tableDrive filesCalendar

Bring any provider yourself with the generic OAuth2 or REST connector — configured entirely from the UI, with named actions and JSON import/export.

Deploy your own in a click.

Bring a Postgres URL and go. Turn on integrations later — the admin tells you exactly what to add.
